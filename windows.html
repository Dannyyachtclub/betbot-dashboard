What the outside research says (cross-check)

Our games are not typical games. Published pricing models put a typical match's one-more-goal price crossing 1.65 around the 62nd minute. Ours almost never does — because Imran's picks are open, already-scoring games where the market expects more goals and holds the price down. Everything on this page describes his picks, not soccer in general.

The push maths checks out. At 55–65', "exactly one more goal" is the single likeliest outcome (~36–37%) — which is why the +1.0 lives on stake-backs, here and in the models alike.

Waiting is punished in print too. The one controlled test the research found on holding out for a better in-play price lost money — games that score during the wait are the ones likeliest to score again. Our Option 6 failure rhymes with it.

The humility line. Published work on 14 seasons of odds warns that a profitable cell in a small sample appears even in efficient markets. Our biggest cell is 19 bets. All of this is description, not proof.
