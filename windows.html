Which Window Pays?

Ranked betting options from our own camp records — every price the bot saw, poll by poll, across 28 games, Nov 21–23. Ranked by what the numbers actually did, not by theory.

The lines, in plain words. The game already has T goals when we camp it. T+0.5 = one more goal wins, nothing back otherwise. T+0.75 = a split line — one more goal wins half, two win it all. T+1.0 = one more goal gives the stake back, two more win. Our rule: nothing is bet under 1.65.

The honest limits

Small numbers. The winning cell rests on 6 graded games; the worst on 2. These describe what happened — they don't prove a pattern yet.

Missing finals. Final scores are unknown for 19 of the 28 games (the settler leaves the field blank), so most qualifications can't be graded. Pulling those finals is already in motion and will sharpen every number on this page.

Soft clock. The recorded match minute can lag the real clock by about a minute, so the window edges are ±1' soft.
The one decision this raises: do we keep camps watching into 67–75' on games we haven't bet — just to build the missing data — or keep ending camps early the way we do now? Everything above is measurement; that choice is yours.
