      In plain words: prices on the charts are real and move live. Everything about the desks — balances, trades, blocks — comes from a snapshot that refreshes about every minute, and says so where it sits. Paper money only: no real orders, no real money.

      How this page works — the full details

      Two layers, labelled where they sit. MARKET: chart prices and the print tape come from the venue's public feed. When the chart shows one of our positions it uses THAT position's own venue first — Bybit USDT-perp websocket for the TD-001 / TD-034 perp positions, then the Coinbase spot stream, then Bybit REST polling, then Binance — because the take-profit is a resting order on that venue, and only that venue's own prices can say whether it was reached. Free browsing starts on Coinbase spot, then Binance. REST polling is labelled polling, never streaming; only when every route fails does the chart fall back to the snapshot's price levels, and it says so. DESK: every desk figure, event and label is rendered from one feed snapshot () on a ~1 min cadence; nothing about the desk is reconstructed or filled in, and the desk layer is never presented as streaming. Sections the snapshot does not carry show NOT YET LIVE with the feed's own reason. SHADOW and PRACTICE labels are the feed's, unedited. Green explosive bursts fire only on a real win — price actually reaching a take-profit level, or a take-profit close on the snapshot tape; red marks a stop, cyan an entry. Depth is one venue's displayed liquidity — advertised interest, not a promise. The flow strip is computed from the venue feed's own aggressor flags. The desk treemap is snapshot data: area is balance, colour is realised P&L, always with the signed figure in the tile. The token search charts any symbol through the same route chain — the position's own venue first for held tokens, then Coinbase, then Binance, then REST polling — and a symbol the venues don't list fails honestly into snapshot mode like any other dead route. AUTO-FOLLOW moves the hero chart to the open trade closest to its entry or its take-profit and states the move on the proximity board and in the chart header; the distances there are live for every streamable symbol and snapshot-marked only where no route streams, labelled per row. Every position row and quick-pick chip reprices from the position feed — Coinbase spot and Bybit perp legs, REST-polled when a websocket dies — with floating P&L recomputed from the position's own entry, quantity and side on every print; only the count-only desks, whose snapshot carries no symbol, stay snapshot-labelled. Every data surface re-renders on the stream event itself — print, book delta, cross — not on a timer; surfaces fed by the desk snapshot keep their snapshot-age labels. In the neural vortex, a pulse travels only on a real event: a live print reaching a market desk, a fill, veto or exit on this snapshot's tape, or a take-profit cross; ABSENT neurons stay dim and dashed. Section banners are artwork of this system — citadel, vault, crucible, core web — labelled as artwork where they sit, never data. Paper only — no real orders, no real money.
